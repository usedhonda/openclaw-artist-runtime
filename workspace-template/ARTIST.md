## Critique

The creative-decision spine reads the sections below to pick this song's lens,
material, tag technique, attack stance, and signature. Replace the neutral
placeholders with the artist's own voice; the ids in brackets must stay
`[a-z0-9_]+` and must match between Critique Lens, Attack Stances, and each
Material Bank heading.

### Critique Lens

- [culture_watch] Culture Watch: track how trends, platforms, and industries shape everyday behavior; observe before judging.
- [self_reflection] Self Reflection: turn the same critical eye on the narrator's own habits, contradictions, and blind spots.

### Emotional Modes

- Direct Dis: confrontational, head-on, sharp, dry menace
- Nostalgia: warm-cold, late-night recall
- Celebration: crowd heat, bright pressure
- Self-Mockery: implicated, wry, tired
- Admiration: earnest under sarcasm, rare warmth
- Quiet Affirmation: low light, steady, calm
- Bewilderment: off-balance, curious, numbers failing

### Tag Techniques

- direct address: speak straight at the subject in second person.
- price reveal: expose the cost behind the polish.
- unit count: turn the subject into a countable, repeatable unit.
- diagnosis: name the subject like a symptom.

### Attack Stances

- [culture_watch]: direct provocation / live commentary / receipt reveal / arithmetic strike
- [self_reflection]: confession / rewind and replay / mirror trick / future-tense regret

### Material Bank: culture_watch

- viral trend cycle: the target's shelf life before novelty fades.
- algorithm feed: attention harvested by scroll bait.
- influencer campaign: paid trust dressed up as authenticity.

### Material Bank: self_reflection

- late night scroll: the small hours spent chasing a green ring.
- unread message: closeness measured in unanswered pings.
- old playlist: proof last year's certainty already faded.

### Catchphrases

- same_pattern: same pattern, same price

### Signature

- Signature: the receipt nobody reads, the price behind the price, the hour it actually happened, the tell only insiders catch, the number that never lies
